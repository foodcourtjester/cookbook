About Successful Experiments with Food

Welcome to my offline-capable recipe book! This PWA is built to store and search recipes quickly without needing an active internet connection.

My journey to creating a catalog of recipes that I use regularly has been a relatively straightforward one. It began out of sheer necessity. The first time I had to live without any family nearby was when I moved from the bustling metropolis of Mumbai, India to the sleepy-college-town-in-the-middle-of-nowhere Clemson, South Carolina. I had three roommates and we were all practically broke, and there were very few decent restaurants anywhere near us. We coulnd't really afford to eat regularly at the ones that were. To save time and money, we decided to pool our resources together and cook dinner for each other - working out a schedule for the semester based on our class meetings. To say I was limited in my abilties in the kitchen would be quite the understatement. For that first year, I primarily cooked one of four recipes in my repertiore - only one of which features on this site (because it is my mother's recipe).

Over time, as I observed friends, neighbors, and new roommates try things out in the kitchen, my own skillset grew. Eventually, when I began to live by myself, I decided to try to recreate, or get as close to, in flavor, and texture, as the food I ordered at restaurants. This personal challenge involved a lot of scouring of the internet, and a lot of failures, but when I did find and successfully recreate a dish, the sheer joy made my endeavors feel very worthwhile.

Mind you, I started this in the very early years of social media, where all of the content featured low quality photos of us and our friends art parties, bars, restaurants, and vacations. My first attempt at baking resulted in only 11 cupcakes in a 12 cupcake tin being filled, and the comments when I proudly posted a picture of it were savage. Over time, though, the quality and consistency of my cooking improved significantly, which led me to start thinking of cataloging the recipes I found easy enough with a flavorful finished product.
