About Successful Experiments with Food

Welcome to my offline recipe book! I built this PWA so you can save, search, and access reliable recipes quickly, even without an internet connection.


My cooking journey started out of necessity when I moved from Mumbai to Clemson, SC as a broke student. With limited kitchen skills, I relied on a rotational cooking schedule with my roommates to survive on a budget. Over the years - fueled by internet deep dives, uncountable kitchen failures, and inspiration from friends, I learned to recreate my favorite restaurant dishes.


After trying various cataloging methods, I think have finally found the best way to list my recipes. This app is my ever-growing personal collection of those successful, flavorful, and reliable recipes.
